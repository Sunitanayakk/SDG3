# Odisha ambulance navigation

Original navigation summary, reviewed against National Health Mission Odisha's
patient transport page on 2026-11-21:
https://nhmodisha.gov.in/patient_transport_2/

In Odisha, the public 108 service provides emergency ambulance transport.
The 108 guidance in this prototype applies specifically to Odisha. The app does
not know whether an ambulance is available and does not book or dispatch one.
Use a deliberate phone call or seek immediate professional help when needed.
For India's broader emergency response service, the national number is 112.
