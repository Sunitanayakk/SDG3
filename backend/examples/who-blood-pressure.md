# Understanding high blood pressure

Original plain-language summary reviewed 2026-11-24.
Source: World Health Organization, Hypertension.
https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hypertension

High blood pressure often has no symptoms. Having blood pressure measured is the
way to know. A qualified healthcare professional can assess readings and discuss
care. Healthy habits and appropriate clinical care matter. Do not start, stop or
change medication based on this summary. It cannot diagnose hypertension or
interpret an individual's measurements.
