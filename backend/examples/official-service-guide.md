# eSanjeevani service navigation

Source organization: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare / C-DAC.
Official portal: https://esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in/
Reference: https://www.cdac.gov.in/index.aspx?id=product_details&productId=eSanjeevaniNationalTelemedicineService
Reviewed for this prototype on 2026-11-17. This is an original navigation summary, not clinical guidance.

eSanjeevani provides government telemedicine access in India. A patient can visit
the official portal to review the registration process and the services currently
offered there. The official portal determines schedules and access requirements.
This prototype cannot confirm clinician availability, reserve appointments or
deliver a consultation. For an urgent medical concern, seek immediate professional
help rather than waiting for an online conversation.
