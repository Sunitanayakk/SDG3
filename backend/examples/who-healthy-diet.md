# General principles of a healthy diet

Original plain-language summary reviewed 2026-11-24.
Source: World Health Organization, Healthy diet.
https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet

A healthy diet is varied and balanced. Vegetables, fruit, pulses and whole grains
are useful parts of a varied diet. Limit excess salt, free sugars and unhealthy
fats. The exact diet depends on individual needs, culture and locally available
foods. A qualified professional can help adapt food choices to a person's needs.
This summary cannot prescribe a diet, treat a condition or recommend medication.
