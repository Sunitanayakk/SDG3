# India emergency service navigation

Original navigation summary, reviewed against the Ministry of Home Affairs ERSS
page on 2026-11-21:
https://www.mha.gov.in/emergency-response-support-system-erss

India uses 112 as the nationwide emergency response number for reporting
emergencies and requesting appropriate assistance. This app only provides
information and a deliberate phone-dialer link. It cannot dispatch assistance,
assess a person's condition or track an emergency response. Anyone who believes
they face an emergency should seek immediate professional help rather than
waiting for an AI answer.
