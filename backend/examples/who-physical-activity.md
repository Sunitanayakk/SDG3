# Physical activity and disability

Original plain-language summary reviewed 2026-11-24.
Source: World Health Organization, Physical activity.
https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity

Physical activity benefits health, including for people living with disability.
Some activity is better than none. Choose movement appropriate to your abilities.
People living with disability can ask a qualified health professional about
adaptations and activities suited to their circumstances. This summary does not
provide a personalised exercise prescription or assess whether activity is safe
for a specific person.
